# Impeccable Design Quality & Craft Floor

## Principles
- **Production-Grade Craft:** Treat all UI/UX with exceptional craft, accessibility (WCAG AA), responsive discipline, and tokenized design systems.
- **Visual Truth & Token Normativity:** Maintain design tokens in `DESIGN.md` (normative frontmatter + canonical 8 sections) and `.impeccable/design.json`.
- **Modes:**
  - *Persuade:* Landing & public portals (engaging, secure, crisp).
  - *Operate:* Administrative & investigative backends (high scanability, predictable data density, clear status indicators).
  - *Read:* Guidelines & FAQ drawers (structured for fast comprehension).
- **Quality Checks:**
  - Form contrast & input accessibility.
  - Complete feedback cycles (loading spinners, empty states, toast notifications, inline errors).
  - No broken wraps, clipping, or missing active states.
